# Portfolio Edition

This repository is a sanitized public representation of the Innovativa SST commercial landing-page package.

Before publication:

- direct commercial phone and WhatsApp numbers were replaced with demonstration values;
- commercial and sender e-mail addresses were moved to deployment placeholders;
- no customer lead data was included;
- no API keys, SMTP passwords or production secrets were included;
- large/private marketing photographs were replaced by portfolio-safe vector illustrations.

The repository preserves the implemented pricing simulator, responsive UI, lead form, FAQ interactions, proposal e-mail architecture and WhatsApp continuation flow.
